Chart Play-Along

Turn your chart into a play along track.

Drop in a song and its chord chart. The builder finds the tempo and the bars, lines the chart's chords up with the recording, and gives you a chart that follows along as the song plays, with a click track you can switch on and download.

Song

MP3, WAV or M4A of the recording.

Chord chart

PDF with chords over lyrics, like a WorshipOnline chart.

No PDF?

    Everything runs in your browser; your files are not uploaded anywhere. Songs recorded to a click (most worship releases) work best. After it builds, check where each section starts. If one is off, click its first bar and set it, and the rest of the song re-lines up around your fix.
